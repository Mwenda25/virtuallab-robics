KitBench offline

1. Double-click 'Start KitBench.bat'. A window opens and your browser loads KitBench.
2. Keep that window open while you use KitBench. Close it to stop.
3. No internet is needed. Everything is in the 'libs' folder.

Windows only. If Windows asks about the firewall, you can cancel: it only runs on your own computer.
Your kit, wiring, lessons and blocks are saved in the browser, per browser.
