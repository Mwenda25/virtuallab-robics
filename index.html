How it works

Write loop(s) and return left and right speeds from -1 to 1. Sensors: s.line.left/mid/right (on the line, or the white sumo edge), s.dist (cm), s.color (colour sensor), s.heading (degrees, IMU, right turn is positive), s.odo (cm driven), s.t (seconds), s.ball (RoboSports pitch with a ball camera: .angle in degrees, .dist in cm, or None when out of view).

A sensor works only if you picked it in Build and wired it correctly. With LEGO colour sensors the line sensors are s.line.left and s.line.right only, and s.line.mid stays false.
